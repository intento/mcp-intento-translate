## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our project seriously. If you believe you've found a security vulnerability, please report it to us by sending an email to security@inten.to.

**Please include the following information in your report:**
- Description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Potential impact of the vulnerability
- Any suggestions for mitigation or fixes (if available)

Our security team will acknowledge your email within 3 working days and will work to verify and respond to your report as quickly as possible. 

After submitting your report, please allow us time to investigate before disclosing the issue publicly. We'll work with you to understand the scope of the issue and will keep you informed of our progress.

**What to expect:**
- Acknowledgment of your report within 3 working days
- Validation and assessment of the reported vulnerability
- Regular updates on our progress (at least weekly)
- Credit for responsibly disclosing the issue (if desired)

## Security Measures

Our project implements the following security measures:

- Regular security audits and code reviews
- Secure development practices following OWASP guidelines
- Data encryption for sensitive information
- Regular dependency updates to address known vulnerabilities

## Security Updates

Security updates will be released as soon as possible after validation and development of a fix. Users are strongly encouraged to update to the latest version to ensure they have all security patches.

## Contact

For any questions about this security policy, please contact us at security@inten.to.
